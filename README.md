# ClarifySQL

**Talk to your database in plain English.**

ClarifySQL turns questions like *"Which department had the most appointments last month?"* into SQL, runs it safely, and explains the answer.

## Features

- **Ask in plain English.** Get the answer, the SQL, and the result table.
- **Schema-checked SQL.** Every query is checked against your real tables and columns before it runs. Bad queries are fixed automatically.
- **Read-only by design.** Built to answer questions, not change your data.
- **Follow-up questions.** The chat remembers the conversation.
- **Instant executive dashboards.** One click suggests KPIs and charts for your data. Add your own widgets in SQL or plain English.
- **Bring your data.** Connect PostgreSQL or Microsoft SQL Server / Azure SQL, or upload a CSV.
- **Private AI option.** Runs with self-hosted open models, so your data can stay on your own servers.
